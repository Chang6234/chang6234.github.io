  • Heavy-Ball Method under Randomized Schedules
    Chang He and Shuzhong Zhang

    In this note, we give a randomized schedule for choosing the stepsize and momentum coefficient of the heavy-ball method, under which it achieves an $\mathcal{O}(1/K^{1.5})$ convergence rate. We allow the momentum coefficient to be larger than 1 instead of restricting it to $(0,1)$. How to design a schedule under which the heavy-ball method attains the optimal $\mathcal{O}(1/K^{2})$ rate remains open.

  • Gradient Descent Achieves $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{-7/4})$ Convergence
    Chang He and Xiaotian Jiang

    In this note, we show that gradient descent can be accelerated for smooth nonconvex optimization by adjusting its stepsizes. When both the gradient and the Hessian are Lipschitz continuous, the optimal gradient complexity of first-order methods is $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{-7/4})$. We prove that, with properly chosen stepsizes, gradient descent alone attains this complexity. The accompanying Lean verification is available at LEAN (.zip).
