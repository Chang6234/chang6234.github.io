Chang He (何畅)

 

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities, advised by Prof. Shuzhong Zhang.

My research interests lie in continuous optimization, with applications in machine learning and operations research. In particular, I work on the following topics:

  • Efficient Optimization Algorithm Design
  • Worst-Case Complexity Analysis
